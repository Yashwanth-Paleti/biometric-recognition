## 3. Empirical Distance Distributions

All facial feature vectors generated by InsightFace Buffalo_L are normalized to unit length:
$$\|\mathbf{u}\|_2 = 1.0, \quad \|\mathbf{v}\|_2 = 1.0$$

The relationship between Euclidean ($L_2$) distance and Cosine Similarity ($\cos \theta$) on the unit sphere is:
$$d_{L_2}^2 = \|\mathbf{u} - \mathbf{v}\|_2^2 = \|\mathbf{u}\|^2 + \|\mathbf{v}\|^2 - 2 \langle \mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v} \rangle = 2 - 2 \cos \theta$$
$$\cos \theta = 1 - \frac{d_{L_2}^2}{2}$$

### Empirical Statistics:

| Distribution | Sample Size | Mean | Std Dev | Min | Median | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Genuine ($d_{gen}$)** | 3 | **0.5608** | 0.2563 | 0.1984 | 0.7378 | **0.7461** |
| **Impostor ($d_{imp}$)** | 25 | **1.3406** | 0.0393 | **1.2658** | 1.3412 | 1.3995 |

### Observation:
Genuine distances cluster between $0.20$ and $0.75$, while impostor distances tightly concentrate around $1.34$ ($\pm 0.04$). The distribution demonstrates zero overlap on this dataset.

- **Corresponding Cosine Similarity:** $\approx 0.639$
- **FAR at 0.85:** $0.00\%$
- **FRR at 0.85:** $0.00\%$
- **Accuracy:** $100.00\%$

### Rationale:
The threshold $0.85$ sits safely within the middle of the separation margin $[0.75, 1.25]$:
- Margin to highest genuine comparison: $0.85 - 0.7461 = 0.1039$ safety buffer.
- Margin to lowest impostor comparison: $1.2658 - 0.85 = 0.4158$ safety buffer.

This allows genuine users to authenticate reliably across lighting variations and slight pose shifts while leaving a large barrier against any impostor match.

## 6. Dataset Limitations & Future Calibration

> [!WARNING]
> **Statistical Significance Disclaimer:**  
> The current evaluation is based on 8 extracted subject embeddings yielding 3 genuine comparisons and 25 impostor comparisons. While this confirms clear empirical separability for the prototype deployment, ISO/IEC 19795 biometric testing standards recommend evaluation across thousands of subjects to compute statistically robust ROC (Receiver Operating Characteristic) curves at $FAR \le 10^{-4}$.

### Future Production Calibration Steps:
1. Benchmark against standardized academic biometric datasets (e.g. LFW, CFP-FP, AgeDB-30).
2. Collect multi-session captures across different cameras, illumination angles, and age cohorts.
3. Maintain threshold configurability via `BIOMETRIC_MATCH_THRESHOLD` environment variable without hardcoding.
